After Class Chat is an unofficial, student-run community. It is not affiliated with or operated by Little Elm ISD. Access is currently limited to invited testers. Test accounts do not establish school-email ownership or verified student identity.

Community rules

  1. Respect the person behind the username. No bullying, harassment, hate speech, discrimination, sexual harassment or threats.
  2. Keep private information private. Do not post real names, email addresses, phone numbers, exact locations, class schedules, passwords or invitation codes. Never pressure someone to share them.
  3. Keep content appropriate for classmates. No sexual content, graphic violence, dangerous challenges or encouragement of harm.
  4. Help people learn. Explain ideas and work through homework together. Do not share stolen exams, answer keys or other people's work as your own.
  5. Keep conversations usable. No spam, scams, impersonation, malicious links, advertising floods or attempts to evade a mute or ban.
  6. Respect boundaries. DMs require accepted friend requests. Stop contacting someone who declines or blocks you. Do not share private conversations without permission.
  7. Protect the community. Share student invitations privately with their intended recipients. Do not trade accounts, exploit bugs or try to enter another grade's chat.
  8. Use the safety tools. Block unwanted contact and report problematic messages or users. Moderators may remove content, mute accounts or suspend access under their assigned permissions.

Reports go to the owner and permitted moderators. This is not a staffed emergency or counselling service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services or a trusted adult rather than waiting for a chat report.

Privacy & your account

  • Profiles: classmates see usernames, grades and roles. Presence is opt-in. Emails are held privately by Supabase Auth; the owner may use an email to look up an existing account for an invitation. Email text alone does not establish identity or grant a role.
  • Conversations: school and subject channels are shared with permitted accounts. Grade channels require the stored grade. DMs/groups require membership and authorization; reporting a message may make that specific message available to permitted moderators.
  • Passwords and sessions: Supabase manages passwords. The site keeps login tokens in this browser tab. Do not share credentials. A one-time email-flow verifier may use shared browser storage. Private testing currently has no email delivery or email-based password recovery.
  • Retention: a daily cleanup is configured to remove messages after 90 days, resolved/dismissed reports after 30 days, and audit records after 90 days. Open reports remain until reviewed. Provider backups and operational logs follow the Supabase project's policies.
  • Controls: open Settings to change presence, enable notifications, log out or delete your chat account. Use a person's profile to mute, block or report them. Grade changes require owner approval.
  • Deletion: deleting a student account removes its profile, friendships, memberships, owned groups and authored message content. Safety records may remain on the retention schedule, with account references removed. Provider backups may persist for their configured retention period. The owner must arrange an operator transfer before deleting the owner account.
  • Games: game progress is separate from chat. Deleting a chat account does not delete saved Garage game progress.
