# Backpacks, drive-out base, Legendary Bank and timed extraction

Implemented:
- Extraction preserves loot, loose equipment, equipped gear and purchased backpack in carried storage. House transfers are explicit. TAB opens backpack; item hover shows description, rarity, quantity/stack and value. Smuggler sells carried items only.
- Packs: starter20 free, courier30/$300, expedition45/$850, vault60/$1800. These capacities count individual units, with visually grouped stacks; they are not stack-slot capacities. Only upgrades can be purchased. Capacity and prices are server-owned, saved with the profile. Elimination loses equipped pack and carried contents, supplies a free starter pack, and preserves secured storage/cash.
- Bottom buildings face north, with connected paths and solid back walls. Base starts seated in the extracted vehicle or a free rental. Garage selection parks a vehicle on the road, preserving its ownership until departure. Drive east past the marked line to deploy; no Depart button. Cars removed from storage only on actual departure and retain prior extraction/permanent-loss rules.
- All eight models have distinct dimensions, original model details, handling/stats, rarity and values. Garage previews call the exact in-game vehicle renderer rather than a generic silhouette.
- Expanded Legendary Bank in the downtown center, gold frontage and field-map marker. Entire footprint is blocked unless that player unlocked it in this city run. Unlock requires criminal role, on foot at the entrance, with a carried Legendary Bank Key. Successful unlock removes exactly one key and alerts police. Repeat unlock requests do not consume another key; another player still requires their own unlock. Access resets on departure/extraction/death. No party access is implemented.
- Every ordinary completed robbery bundle is eligible for one independent `< .005` server roll. A successful roll replaces that bundle with a single key (no overflow or extra roll on a full bag). Bank rewards are excluded. Keys are server-created valuables, transferable/extractable/storable and lost on elimination. Key drop chance is 0.5% per eligible completed bundle, not a guaranteed key after200 bundles. Bank loot starts at$1000 with higher reward growth than ordinary stores, security7, health pressure, timed robbery and alerts.
- Extraction is automatic after8 continuous simulated seconds inside the65-unit exit circle, for criminals and off-duty civilians. Leaving resets the timer. Officers cannot extract or use the home-return shortcut; E at the police station ends duty, restores personal equipment and allows extraction. Progress renders smoothly from acknowledged/replayed inputs and stops below completion until authority confirms.

Prior test fixtures were updated for seated base spawns, road deployment and carried extraction rather than the removed menu workflow.

Initial fixtures place one criminal at extraction and the other at the bank; this is not proof of driving the entire route between them. No page errors.
- Separate two-context browser extraction check tests leaving/re-entering the circle, automatic completion with carried loot, and officer station interaction. Server tests additionally prove a still-on-duty officer cannot complete extraction.
- Hosting package checks migration journal and Worker startup. Wallet migration/shop regression tests passed. No other game simulation files or database schemas changed. Existing saves remain canonical arrays; added profile fields default safely. Static model definitions keep the same world ID because city shape is preserved aside from the authored bank expansion. Rejoining players caught in expanded bank walls recover to a roadside.
