A QUICK BRIEFING

Welcome aboard,
Captain.

Cargo runs start at the crane: use A/D to move the trolley, W/S to raise/lower the hook, and Space to grab or release a dock container. Lift clear of the stack and place all six boxes on the cargo deck. They slide to rest without snapping into slots; pick a box up again with Space to adjust your stack. Then press E to sail. Hold E to let cruise passengers walk up the gangway. Release E to pause boarding. Sail right to Northstar Pier, stop inside the berth, then hold E to deliver.

WASD · Space
While loading cargo: A/D moves the crane, W/S hoists the hook, Space grabs/releases. Trolley momentum makes the load swing. Boxes dropped during loading are recovered to the dock. At sea, waves can slide badly balanced boxes overboard. Each lost container removes one-sixth of this voyage’s completion bonus.
← / A · → / D
Reverse / sail forward. Up or W also sails forward.
↓ / S
Brake. Slow boats take less damage from big waves.
Aim + click
Cargo ship: shoot at pirate boats. No ammunition limit.
Hold F
Cargo ship: water cannons repel nearby pirates on both sides. Release to recharge pressure.
Tap Space
Cruise ship: restart a failed engine. Tap repeatedly; holding the key does not count.
Hold E
Board passengers, depart after loading cargo, deliver, or rescue. For a rescue, brake with S, hold A or ← to reverse back to the orange life ring, then stop and hold E before the timer runs out.
Hold R
Stop and repair for 3 seconds. Each kit restores up to 35% hull.
Esc / P
Pause your voyage.

Harder waters bring more incidents, larger waves, and more tsunamis. Protect the hull, repel boarding pirates, and bring every passenger home. Touch controls are also available.
