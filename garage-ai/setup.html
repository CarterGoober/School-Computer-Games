Use Garage AI on the web

Open study chat, sign in with your Garage account, and ask a question. Add a screenshot using the regular file picker, review it and confirm before sending. No installation, browser extension, Developer Mode or screen-capture permission is required. Automatic mode analyzes only content you explicitly add and confirm.

This web-first update is prepared locally for owner review. AI remains unavailable until the owner confirms a Cloudflare Workers Free plan and approves deployment. Free-tier availability and quotas depend on the providers; there is no promise of unlimited free usage.

Optional extension for permitted devices

  1. Get the unpacked build folder from the project owner. No Chrome Web Store release is available yet.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select garage-ai/unpacked.
  3. Open the extension’s Details → Extension options and sign in with your existing Garage school account.
  4. On a supported webpage, click the Garage AI toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+G.
  5. Click Analyze Screen, review the preview, discard sensitive images, then explicitly Send your question.

Managed school devices may block installation or capture. Ask your administrator; do not bypass those restrictions.

Privacy & cost

Manual capture is the default. Automatic mode only prepares a preview when opened; it never sends it automatically. Confirmed images and conversation text go to Cloudflare Workers AI through Supabase. The gateway does not intentionally log prompts or images. Provider retention depends on the Cloudflare account configuration and provider policy; this is not a zero-retention promise. Local history is optional, text-only and removable. Usage counters are pruned when the account is active; inactive rows remain until later activity or account deletion. There are no student charges, subscriptions or credit purchases.

Never upload passwords, payment details or private messages. Use AI only where your assignment rules allow it.
