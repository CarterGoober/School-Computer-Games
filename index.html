HOW TO PLAY

Mouse. Keys.
Mischief.

Open the arena, claim a runner name, pick a loadout, then choose 1v1, 2v2 or 5v5. When enough runners are queued the server starts a match. If the plaza is quiet, practice bots fill empty slots after a short wait so you are never stuck staring at a queue.

Progress is stored server-side. Guests can play immediately, then link a name and password to keep unlocks across devices.
