WELCOME TO THE GARAGE

A little escape.
Always in your pocket.

Four original games: Tideline for cargo deliveries, cruise missions, pirates, and rough seas; Apex Circuit for timed racing; and Sunday Slide for suburban drifting, harbor jumps, and a slightly ridiculous train.

To install on a Chromebook, wait for OFFLINE READY, then choose Install Afterclass Garage in Chrome’s menu, or use the install button when available. Launch it from your Chromebook Launcher to play in its own app window, even without internet.

First load requires an allowed internet connection. Managed devices may restrict installation. Best laps stay on this device. The SF90 is a stylized fan tribute; this game is not affiliated with Ferrari. Sunday Slide has original art and tracks; it is not affiliated with Over the Hill.
