THE PLAYBOOK

Five linemen.
One set of keys.

  1. Read the QB call. 22 runs right. 23 runs left. 55 protects the passer.
  2. Snap with SPACE. Hit the gold center for PERFECT. The target changes every play.
  3. Press the assignment arrow after the snap. → for 22, ← for 23, ↓ for 55. Speed matters. A wrong first assignment locks in.
  4. React to the pressure prompt. Match the arrow before its timer runs out. Edge rushers need a side pickup; inside pressure needs ↓. A fake blitz calls for ↓ to hold the pocket.
  5. Stay on your feet. Correct calls and active reactions improve leverage. Low leverage lets defenders shed blocks and reach the QB.

Practice drills last 5 reps. Grades gradually add up to +8 gameplay rating points. All base ratings start at 70; these are fictional game values, never evaluations of real students.

Games use four 90-second arcade quarters, 4 downs to gain 10 yards, automatic extra points, safeties, punts and short-range field goals on 4th down. Opposing possessions are simulated. Ties stand. There is no playable defense or multiplayer in this release.

Escape pauses. Switching tabs pauses. Touch players can use the on-screen keys. Saves belong to this browser; they do not sync between devices.
